# TrackMyTime

A simple office-hours tracker by **Nikkhil Deshmukkh**.

Open it in Microsoft Edge: https://keedaaa.github.io/TrackMyTime/

- Records when your laptop is on, breaks, lunch and time away
- 9 hours a day, 5 days a week (changeable), with ahead / behind reports
- Holidays, leave, weekly and monthly reports
- Your hours are saved inside Edge on your own laptop. Nothing is sent anywhere.
